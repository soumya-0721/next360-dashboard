About Next360

Our story, our mission, and the people behind India's organic revolution.

Our Mission

To make certified organic products accessible to every Indian household, ensuring purity, sustainability, and affordability at every step.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted organic brand, setting global standards for quality, sustainability, and community impact.

Our Story

In 2018, what started as a small team of passionate organic enthusiasts in Hyderabad has grown into one of India's fastest-growing organic product companies. Our founder recognized that while demand for organic products was rising, trust and accessibility remained major barriers.

We set out to change that — building a vertically integrated supply chain that works directly with farmers, ensures rigorous quality testing, and delivers products that families can trust completely.

Today, Next360 serves over 10 million customers across 50+ cities, with a product range spanning organic foods, health supplements, personal care, and home essentials. Every product we create reflects our unwavering commitment to purity, quality, and sustainability.
